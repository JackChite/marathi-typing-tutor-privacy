Marathi Typing Tutor ("the App") is an educational typing-practice application developed by Star Cheetah Studio. This Privacy Policy explains the information the App processes, how local practice data is stored, and how Google AdMob advertising is provided.

1. Information Processed Locally

The App processes practice-related information locally on your Android device, including lesson progress, completed lessons, WPM, gross WPM, net WPM, CPM, accuracy, correct/incorrect/total counts, backspaces, duration, stars, timestamps, daily practice records and streak-related information. Preferences include keyboard layout, font size, difficulty, sound settings, dark mode, finger guide, virtual keyboard visibility and WPM calculation method. These practice records and preferences are stored locally using the App's Room database and related local storage.

2. How Local Information Is Used

Local practice information is used to provide typing-practice features, calculate statistics, save lesson progress, maintain practice history and remember preferences. The App does not require an account or login and does not upload typing transcripts or practice statistics to Star Cheetah Studio.

3. Physical Keyboard Input

When a physical keyboard is connected through Android/USB OTG, keyboard input is processed locally for typing practice and accuracy calculation. The App does not upload the user's typing practice text to Star Cheetah Studio.

4. Advertising and Google Mobile Ads

The App integrates Google Mobile Ads SDK version 23.6.0 and displays an anchored adaptive banner ad on the Home screen. The banner is served by Google AdMob. The App does not currently include Firebase, a separate analytics SDK, a separate crash-reporting SDK, or a Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent flow. The App also does not provide an in-app advertising-personalization control. Advertising preferences and consent choices that Google or Android make available may depend on the user's region, device, account, and applicable settings.

When the Mobile Ads SDK requests and serves ads, Google may automatically collect or receive information as described in Google's documentation for the relevant SDK version and configuration. Categories may include IP address (which may be used to estimate general/approximate location), ad or app interactions, diagnostic information, Android Advertising ID, app set ID, and other device or account identifiers where applicable. Google may use relevant information for ad delivery and measurement, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and compliance. Data handling can depend on SDK behavior, device settings, and consent or privacy choices that apply.

For Google's current disclosure details, see Google Mobile Ads data disclosure for Google Play. The App's Google Mobile Ads SDK version is 23.6.0; refer to the applicable documentation and configuration when interpreting these categories.

5. Network Access and Offline Operation

The App requests the Android INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE permissions to support Google AdMob banner requests and network connectivity checks. Advertising requires a network connection and may not always load. Core typing-practice functionality is designed to remain available offline. The App does not request storage/media or direct USB-access permissions solely for the typing-practice workflow.

6. Data Sharing and Third-Party Processing

Google Mobile Ads processes advertising-related data under Google's services and applicable terms and privacy disclosures. This may involve transmitting data from the device to Google or other advertising partners as described in Google's disclosure. The App developer does not receive or store the user's typing transcripts or practice statistics on a developer-operated server.

7. Local Storage, Retention and Deletion

Practice statistics, lesson progress, history and preferences are stored locally on the Android device. Reset Progress clears lesson progress/statistics but retains preferences. Clearing the App's storage removes local records and preferences. Local data otherwise remains until it is reset, cleared, removed by uninstall/app storage actions, or otherwise handled by Android.

8. Android Backup

Android backup is enabled for the App. Depending on the user's Android/device and backup-provider settings, Android may back up or transfer application data and may later restore it. This system-level backup is separate from the App uploading data to Star Cheetah Studio. Backup copies are controlled by Android and the user's configured backup settings.

9. Consent and Advertising Choices

The current App does not include a Google UMP consent flow or an in-app control for advertising personalization. Do not interpret this policy as claiming that a consent flow is implemented. Google, Android, or applicable regional requirements may provide privacy or advertising choices outside the App. The developer should ensure that the App's consent and advertising configuration meets the requirements that apply to its distribution regions and audience.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is an educational typing-practice application and does not provide an account/profile system for submitting personal information to the developer. The App does use third-party advertising. A parent or guardian should review the applicable Google advertising and privacy disclosures when considering the App for a child. The developer does not intentionally collect typing transcripts or practice statistics from children on a developer-operated server.

11. Data Security

Local application data is subject to the security of the Android device and its operating-system controls. Google Mobile Ads data transmitted by the SDK is handled according to Google's applicable security practices and disclosures. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's functionality or privacy practices change. The "Last updated" date will be revised when material changes are published.
