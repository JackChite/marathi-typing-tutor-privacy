Privacy Policy for Marathi Typing Tutor

Last updated: September 9, 2026

Marathi Typing Tutor ("the App") is a typing-practice application developed by JackChite. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App processes, where it is stored, and how it is used.

1. Information We Process

The App may process the following information locally on your Android device:

  • Typing practice input entered through a connected physical keyboard.
  • Lesson progress and completed lessons.
  • Practice statistics such as typing speed (WPM/CPM), accuracy, errors, characters, and practice duration.
  • Practice history and dates used for progress and streak information.
  • App preferences such as selected settings, sound preferences, theme, and keyboard/practice preferences.

2. How Information Is Used

This information is used only to provide the App's typing-practice features, calculate typing statistics, save progress, maintain practice history, and remember your preferences.

3. Physical Keyboard Input

When you use a physical keyboard connected to your Android device, the App processes keyboard input locally for typing practice and accuracy calculation. Keyboard input is not sent to our servers by the App.

4. Data Collection and Sharing

Marathi Typing Tutor does not require an account or login. The App does not intentionally transmit the practice data described above to a remote server, and we do not sell, rent, or share that data with third parties.

5. Internet and Third-Party Services

The App is designed to work offline for its core typing-practice features. The App does not include advertising or analytics services that intentionally collect your personal information.

6. Data Storage

Practice progress, statistics, history, and preferences are stored locally on your Android device. This information remains under your control through the device and Android app storage mechanisms.

7. Data Deletion

You can remove the App and its locally stored application data using Android's app management settings. Any locally stored practice information is also subject to the device's normal storage and backup mechanisms.

8. Children's Privacy

The App does not intentionally collect personal information from children or other users. Because the App does not require an online account and does not intentionally transmit practice data to our servers, no user profile is created by the App.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to keep the App's locally stored information within the Android application environment. However, no electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's functionality or data practices change. Any updated version will be published at this page with a new "Last updated" date.
